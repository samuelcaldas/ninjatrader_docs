Using Time & Sales Quotes

[Show/Hide Hidden Text]()

The NinjaTrader Time & Sales has the ability to add additional quotes for further analysis for real-time market prices.

 

 

##         [Understanding the Quotes window]()

##

##

| The Quotes window is used to add, remove and edit quotes within a Time & Sales <br> <br>Accessing the Quotes Window<br>Right mouse click on the Time & Sales window and select the menu Quotes <br> <br>Sections of the Quotes Window<br>The image below displays the three sections of the Quotes window:<br>1.List of Available quotes2.Current quotes Configured on the Time & Sales3.Selected columns Properties <br><br><br>![T&S_1](../images/t&s_1.png) |
| --- |

##         [How to add quotes]()

##

| Adding a Quote<br>To add an Quote to a Time & Sales:<br>1. Open the Quotes window (see the "Understanding the Quotes window" section above)<br>2. Left mouse click on the Available quote you want to add and press the Add button or simply double click on it<br>3. The quote will now be visible in the list of Configured quotes<br>4. The quote's parameters will now be editable on the right side of the quotes window<br> <br><br><br>![T&S_2](../images/t&s_2.png) |
| --- |

##         [How to remove quotes]()

##

| Removing a Quote<br>To remove a quote from your NinjaTrader Time & Sales:<br> <br>•Open the Quotes window (see the "Understanding the Quotes window" section above), select a quote from the Configured columns list, press the Remove button, and then press the OK button to exit the Quotes window. |
